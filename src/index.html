The Layer 1 blockchain for travel

Camino Network is an EVM-compatible blockchain built for the travel industry. Validators are KYB-verified travel companies, fees are low and predictable, and transactions settle in seconds.

Camino Messenger
One connection to search, book and pay

Camino Messenger is a B2B transactional distribution layer for travel: suppliers and distributors connect once and trade through standard booking and payment messages.
