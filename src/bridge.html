Camino Bridge Tokens
Transfer Tokens Across
Blockchain Networks

Seamlessly bridge tokens between Ethereum and Camino Network using our simple bridge widget. Connect your wallet and transfer tokens in just a few clicks.

How It Works
  1. Connect your Web3 wallet (MetaMask, etc.)
  2. Select the source chain (Ethereum or Camino)
  3. Enter the token amount you want to bridge
  4. Select the target chain
  5. Confirm the transaction in your wallet
Requirements
  • Modern browser with Web Components support
  • Web3 wallet extension installed
  • Sufficient balance for gas fees
  • Tokens available on the source chain
Security & Fees

All bridge transactions are secured by smart contracts and verified by the network validators. Bridge fees are calculated based on network conditions and gas prices at the time of transaction.

Always verify the transaction details in your wallet before confirming. Double-check the source and target chains to ensure you're bridging to the correct network.
