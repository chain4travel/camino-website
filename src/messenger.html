Camino Messenger continues as Travel Token Messenger

Camino Messenger is a B2B transactional distribution layer for travel, first built on Camino Network: suppliers and distributors connect once, then search, book and pay through the same connection. Its development continues as Travel Token Messenger, open source under LGPLv3.

Camino Messenger provides the transaction layer that powers AI agents to move beyond planning and recommendations — to actually complete bookings, handle payments, and coordinate suppliers in real time.

Trusted ecosystem transactions

Multi-party coordination

How the Messenger works
1
Partner Plugin: connect once

A partner plugin translates a company's booking system into the Messenger protocol: versioned Protocol Buffers message types for accommodation, transport, activities and product lists. Each company integrates once and reaches every counterparty that speaks the same protocol.

2
Bot and Matrix: one bot per company

Each company runs its own bot, on its own infrastructure. The bot holds the company's keys, signs every request and response, and exchanges them with other bots over Matrix, an end-to-end encrypted, federated transport.

3
CMAccount: identity on-chain

Every participant has a smart-contract account (CMAccount) that lists its bots, the services it offers and its payment settings. Counterparties verify it on-chain before they trade.

4
Booking token: search, book, cancel

A distributor searches many suppliers in parallel, validates the chosen offer, then books it. The booking is minted as a token, so delivery and payment happen together; cancellations follow the same path.

5
Cheques: pay per request

Distributors pay suppliers per request with signed cheques (EIP-712) that accumulate off-chain and settle on-chain in batches, so high request volumes do not congest the chain. Settlement runs in stablecoins such as EURe or in the network token.

Summary

Camino Network does not replace aggregators; it redefines the concept of aggregation. It transforms a closed, company-owned model into an open, industry-owned infrastructure, where every participant benefits from shared connectivity, transparent rules, and programmable transactions.

This shift moves aggregation from a commercial service to a strategic industry capability, enabling travel companies to control their distribution, costs, and innovation pace collectively for the first time.

{% image "./src/static/images/messenger-use-case-01.png", "", "", "object-contain mb-8" %}
Challenge:

Italian hotels seek to attract more German tourists to grow their bookings and increase revenue. To achieve this, they need a streamlined way to connect with German tour operators who can facilitate access to this specific market.

Solution:

Camino Messenger is a free, easy-to-setup, and inexpensive platform. Hotels can connect with tour operators without complex software setups and start trading directly via the “accommodation” message type.

{% image "./src/static/images/messenger-use-case-02.png", "", "", "object-contain mb-8" %}
Challenge:

A surf school in Bali is looking to expand its reach and sell surf classes to a global audience of tourists. The school aims to attract tourists from various countries interested in surfing lessons during their stay in Bali.

Solution:

Camino Messenger provides the surf school with a defined “activity” message type to seamlessly connect and trade with airlines that bring tourists to Bali. The surf school (supplier) gets higher sales, the airline (distributor) widens the range of services.

{% image "./src/static/images/messenger-use-case-03.png", "", "", "object-contain mb-8" %}
Challenge:

A charter airline company with 5 aircraft (from 6 to 20 passengers) operates from airports in DACH region. 50% of sale go through resellers. 50% — through direct marketing and recommendation. The airline aims to simplify payment processes and reduce complications from fees to last-minute changes and cancellations.

Solution:

Connecting with Camino Messenger, the airline transforms the distribution flow. Smart contracts automate payments and eliminate disputes. Now, the free resources can be redirected toward new customer experiences and a stronger brand identity to increase direct sales.
